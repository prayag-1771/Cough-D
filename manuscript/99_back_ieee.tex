\section*{Acknowledgment}

The authors thank the authors of the three corpora used here for releasing them publicly,
and in particular for publishing the material that makes their limitations visible: the
participant identifiers that allow a speaker disjoint partition to be built, the detector
scores that make the provenance of a label explicit rather than hidden, and the
exhaustively verified evaluation split that allows an independent test of a detector
trained elsewhere.

\section*{Data Availability}

All three corpora analyzed in this study are publicly available and none were collected by
the authors. The prompt derived corpus is distributed by its authors~\cite{coswara2020}.
The classifier derived corpus is distributed through Zenodo, and version~3 of its public
release was used~\cite{coughvid2021}. The human annotated corpus is distributed through
Zenodo, and only its evaluation split was used~\cite{fsd50k2022}. The participant
assignments, image sets and decoded caches derived from them are not redistributed, since
all are regenerated by the ingest scripts from the original releases.

\section*{Code Availability}

The complete pipeline, the per recording decision files, and the scripts that generate
every table and figure are available at
\url{https://github.com/prayag-1771/Cough-D}. Every number reported in this article is
computed from the decision files in that repository by the scripts in it, rather than
transcribed.

\section*{Ethics Statement}

This study is a secondary analysis of publicly available, de-identified audio corpora and
involved no collection of new data from human participants. Approval and participant
consent for the original collections were the responsibility of the respective corpus
authors. No individually identifiable data or recordings are reproduced in this article.
