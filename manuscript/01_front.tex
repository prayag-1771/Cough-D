\title[Prompted labels and easy negatives inflate cough detection accuracy]{Prompted
labels and easy negatives inflate cough detection accuracy: a controlled comparison
across three label regimes}

\author[1]{\fnm{Prayag} \sur{Sharma}}\email{prayag.sharma2024@vitstudent.ac.in}

\author*[2]{\fnm{Ravi} \sur{Tiwari}}\email{ravi.tiwari@vit.ac.in}

\abstract{Reported accuracies for automatic cough detection sit in the high nineties, yet
audits report that such models are seldom validated outside their development corpus and
often separate the classes by reading a confound. Which part is responsible has not been
isolated, because label provenance, the difficulty of the negative class and run to run
variation ordinarily move together. This study holds the preprocessing, the architecture
and the training protocol fixed and varies the provenance of the label. Three corpora are
used: one labelled by what a participant was asked to do, one labelled by a human listener
against an audio ontology, and one labelled by the score of another classifier. The class
prior is matched across all three, partitions are built over participants so that no
speaker crosses a split, every condition is repeated under five seeds, and conditions are
compared by McNemar's test on paired per recording decisions rather than on summary
accuracies. Trained and tested within the prompted corpus, a ResNet18 reaches 98.6 per
cent balanced accuracy on held out speakers. The same weights applied without retraining
to human annotated audio at the same prior reach 70.2 per cent, and the loss is
asymmetric: specificity falls to 84.3 per cent while sensitivity falls to 56.0. Wavelet
shrinkage does not recover it, and removes a median of 0.67 per cent of signal power, so
the inputs it produces differ very little from the originals. The external set is small
and single source, so the size of the fall is estimated less precisely than its
direction.}

\keywords{Audio classification, External validation, Wavelet shrinkage, Respiratory sound
analysis, Dataset bias}
