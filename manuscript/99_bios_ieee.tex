the National Institute of Technology, Rourkela, India, in 2014 and 2019, respectively. He

Senior Assistant Professor in the Department of Electronics and Communication, Madanapalle
Institute of Technology and Science (MITS), Madanapalle, Andhra Pradesh, from May 2019 to
January 2022. Since January 2022, he has been an Associate Professor with the School of
Electronics Engineering (SENSE), Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT), Chennai, India.
His research interests include statistical signal processing, wireless communication, and
information theory. He has published in reputed journals such as IEEE Transactions, IEEE
Letters, and Wiley Transactions.
